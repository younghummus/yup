Same night ocean, full moon. Camera locked just above the water surface, completely still. The winged armoured valkyrie rockets past the camera from the far background to the foreground and out of frame in under two seconds, skimming a few metres above the sea. Her colossal wings, spanning four times her height, cast a huge shadow across the water. The force of her flight rips the ocean apart beneath her into a sharp V-shaped wake, towering walls of spray peeling away on both sides along her entire path. Golden radiant light and lightning trail off her body. Whip-fast motion, motion blur, camera shake as she passes, deafening roar of wind.
