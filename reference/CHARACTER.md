# Valkyrie — character sheet

Paste the **identity block** into every shot prompt (after the scene line) and attach
`character/01-front-night-portal.jpg` as the primary image/reference so she stays consistent.

## Identity block

> A tall athletic female valkyrie in a polished gold Spartan-style full helmet with a
> T-shaped visor and small golden feathered wings rising from its sides; long wild wavy
> strawberry-blonde hair streaming from under the helmet; a white scale-textured
> breastplate with a bold crimson V chevron trimmed in gold; gold layered shoulder
> pauldrons and a gold belt; gold segmented bracers; a short crimson pleated battle
> skirt flecked with gold; gold knee-high armoured greaves and sabatons. Colossal
> white feathered wings with warm golden backlight glowing through the feathers.

## Do not change
- Helmet always on, face hidden behind the visor.
- Palette: white + crimson + gold armour; white wings; golden light and lightning.
- Wings are white feathers, never black, metallic, or energy-only.
